10.4 Mechanical energy
The sum of the kinetic energy and the
potential energy of an object is called its
mechanical energy.
Consider the following example.
The kinetic energy of an aeroplane at
rest is zero. Its potential energy when it is
on ground is also considered as zero.  Thus
its mechanical energy is zero while it rests
on the ground.  When the same aeroplane
flies it has kinetic energy as well as
potential energy, the sum of these energies
gives the total mechanical energy of the
aeroplane in flight.
