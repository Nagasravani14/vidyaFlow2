Philosophers of the ancient world were

concluded  that the natural state of an
earthly object is to be at rest. He thought
that the object at rest requires no
explanation as any moving body naturally
comes to rest.
Galileo Galilee stated that an object in

farther the ball travelled. He extended this
argument and concluded that if the surface
was perfectly smooth, the ball will travel
indefinitely, until encountered by another
object. (In real world such a surface of

Laws of motion, Inertia, Mass, Linear momentum, Conservation of momentum,
Impulse, Impulsive force
l First Law of Motion: A body continues its state of rest or of uniform motion unless a
net force acts on it.
l The natural tendency of objects to resist a change in their state of rest or of uniform
motion is called inertia.
l The mass of an object is a measure of inertia. SI unit of mass is Kilogram (kg).
l Second Law of Motion: The rate of change of momentum of a body is directly
proportional to the net force acting on it and it takes place in the direction of net
force.
