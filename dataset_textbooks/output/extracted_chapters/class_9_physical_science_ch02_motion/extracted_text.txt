Key words
l Motion is relative.  Motion of an object depends on the observer.
l Distance is the path length traversed and displacement is the shortest distance in a
specified direction.
l Average speed is distance covered per unit time and average velocity is displacement in
a specified direction per unit time.
l Speed at an instant is instantaneous speed which gives the idea of how fast the position
of the body changes.
l Velocity is speed in specified direction.
l The motion is uniform when the velocity is constant.
l A body has acceleration when the velocity of the body changes.
l Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.
l The motion is said to be uniform accelerated motion if acceleration is constant.
