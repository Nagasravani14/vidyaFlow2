What  we have discussed
• Figures are said to be congruent if they have same shape and size.
•
Figures are said to be similar if they have same shapes but with different size.
• If we flip, slide or rotate the congruent/similar shapes their congruence/similarity
remains the same.
• Some figures may have more than one line of symmetry.
• Symmetry is of three types namely line symmetry, rotational symmetry and
point symmetry.
• With rotational symmetry, if the figure is rotated around a central point, it appears
the same as original two or more times. The number of times it appears as the
same, is called it’s order of symmetry.
• The method of drawing enlarged or reduced similar figures is called Dilation.
• The patterns formed by repeating figures to fill a plane without gaps or overlaps
are called tessellations.
