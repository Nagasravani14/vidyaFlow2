organisms have different needs. They
usually stay in the places where most of
their needs are met, that is, they get
sufficient food, shelter and other

All organisms depend on their

water, air and shelter. The surrounding
which meets the needs of a particular

What we have learnt
• Habitat is a dwelling place  for plants
and animals that gives them optimum
conditions for  life.
• Tree, pond, house are some examples
of habitats.
• Temperature, moisture, air, water, food,
shelter are the components of a habitat.
Aquatic habitats come in many forms: lakes, wetlands, lagoons, streams, rivers
and swamps.
