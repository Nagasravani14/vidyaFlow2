Now let us understand  the logic behind the  Sudha’s trick
Suppose Raju chooses the number 10a + b (such that “a” is a digit in tens place and “b” is a
digit in units place and  a ≠  0) can be written as   10 × a + b =  10a + b and  on reversing
the digits  he gets the number 10b + a. When he adds the two numbers he gets (10a + b ) +
(10b + a)  = 11a + 11b = 11(a + b)
The sum is always multiple of 11.  Observe that if she divides the sum by 11 , the quotient is
(a + b), which is exactly the sum of digits  a and b  of chosen number.
